# Premier League Predictive Dashboard (2026/27)

An executive-grade, multi-page Power BI dashboard built on top of a predictive Premier League dataset.

## Pages Included
- **Page 1: Season Overview** — Macro-level predictive end-of-season point projections and team standings.
- **Page 2: Offensive Overview** — Tactical breakdown of goal volumes, top scorer distributions, and performance trends.
